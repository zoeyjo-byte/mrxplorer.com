A few things you might be wondering.

Can I start if I barely use AI?

Yes. We’ll start with your questions and build at a pace that works for you. You don’t need technical experience or a particular job title.

What if I already use AI regularly?

We’ll focus on where you want to improve: more reliable outputs, repeatable workflows, evaluating tools, or exploring agents and automation. Our first conversation will help establish whether your goals fit my expertise.

Can my employer pay for coaching?

Yes. If you’re seeking employer support, we can outline the goals and scope of your engagement so you can discuss it with your manager.

Can we work with my research projects?

Yes, where your organization’s policies and permissions allow it. We can also use redacted or example materials. We’ll discuss confidentiality and appropriate AI use before working with sensitive information.

What happens after three months?

We’ll review your progress and decide what would be useful next. You can continue monthly with new goals, or finish when you’ve accomplished what you came to do.

What would you like to do better with AI?

You don’t need a polished plan. Bring a question, a challenge, or an idea. We’ll start there.
